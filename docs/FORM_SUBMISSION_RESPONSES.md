### 8. Describe your project in 2–3 sentences. *
```text
RealityCheck is an autonomous reconciliation agent that acts as "Git diff for real life," ensuring what counterparties deliver matches what was promised in emails, receipts, and subscriptions. It compiles agreements into machine-verifiable Expectation Contracts, deterministically isolates discrepancies against later bills or outcomes, and takes permissioned corrective action with user consent. Rather than closing when a provider promises a refund, it creates a monitored OWED obligation and marks the case recovered only when verified proof arrives.
```
